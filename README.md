# solar
Solar: the live wind from the Sun, streaming as smoke past a turning Earth and parting around its magnetic shield.

Press Begin or the space bar to start, and space again to pause. The wind's speed, density and temperature shape the smoke; its pressure and magnetic field size the shield and brighten the auroras. Data from the [NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/), map from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). `tools/make-land.py` rebuilds `land.js`.
